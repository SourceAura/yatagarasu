# Yatagarasu

The Kasugai scroll, seated as a Ryoku notification card.

Every notification arrives as a dispatch scroll carried by the
three-legged crow of Amaterasu: a dark glass plate with a lit
priority edge — cyan for low, emerald for normal, rose for
critical — the message set in mono, and a burn line along the
foot that spends the scroll's remaining life while it waits.

A critical scroll does not burn. It waits, shivering faintly,
with ACK REQUIRED standing in its meta row, until the ✕ is
pressed. The crow perches at the card's corner as its seal;
her prism eye burns rose while a critical waits.

Actions the sender offered appear as a quiet row of chips.
Clicking the plate runs the sender's default action.
