# Yatagarasu Roost

**Retired.** The crow lives in the notification card now; see
the Yatagarasu README one folder up. This piece is no longer
listed or maintained. The folder stays in this repo for
reference.

What follows is the Roost's own README, as it stood.

The crow herself, roosting on your desktop.

Yatagarasu was two listings under one name: the notification
design, and this plugin, the crow herself. She stands on her
prism wire on the desktop, and her eye wears the colour of
the last notification the wire carried: LOW cyan, MEDIUM
emerald, HIGH amber, CRITICAL rose; the calm prism when the
wire is clear.

She hears the desktop the plain way: her own listener
(bin/monitor.py, a few lines of Python) watches the session's
notification bus and keeps the mood. No shell internals, no
compositor, nothing privileged.

Part of the Jurai suite, by 0xSrcAura.
